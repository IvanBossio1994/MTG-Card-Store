The service

TCG Store Helper is a tool to manage the inventory, prices, cash register and reservations of a trading card store, using each user's own Google Sheet. There is a free plan with a card limit and a paid plan that removes it.

Your data

Your data is yours and stays in your Google Sheet and on your computer. You are responsible for keeping backups of your Sheet. See the privacy policy.

Reference prices

The prices shown by the app come from public sources and may contain errors or delays. Check your prices before selling: the final pricing decision is yours.

Paid plan

The Store plan is billed monthly through Lemon Squeezy and can be cancelled at any time. After cancelling, the app keeps working with the free plan limit and no data is lost.

Warranty

The app is provided "as is". We do our best for it to work well, but we are not liable for losses arising from its use, from outages of third-party services (Google, price sources) or from errors in the data.
