Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Summary

TCG Store Helper is an application that runs on your own computer. We have no servers that store your inventory, your sales or your customers: all of that stays in your Google Sheet and on your computer.

What Google data we use

If you choose to connect your Google account, the app requests only these permissions:

  • openid and email: to show which account you are signed in with.
  • drive.file: to create your inventory spreadsheet and read and write to it. This permission only gives access to files the app itself creates or opens, not to the rest of your Google Drive.

The app uses this data only to work on your computer: showing your account and keeping your inventory, cash register and reservations in your Sheet. We do not sell it, we do not share it with third parties, we do not use it for advertising and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

TCG Store Helper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

  • Your inventory, movements, cash register and reservations: in your Google Sheet, in your account.
  • The Google access permission (token) and your settings: in the app's data folder, on your computer.

External services

To show prices, the app queries public card price lists (Card Kingdom and Scryfall for Magic; TCGdex and pokemontcg.io for Pokémon; YGOPRODeck for Yu-Gi-Oh!). Those requests only send card data (name, set and number), never data about you or your customers.

If you activate the Store plan, the license key is validated with our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy). They handle billing and the purchase email under their own privacy policy.

How to delete your data or remove access

  • In the app: Settings, Google account, Disconnect. This revokes the permission and deletes the local token.
  • From Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your Sheet is yours: you can delete it or keep it from your Google Drive.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to tcgstorehelper@gmail.com.
