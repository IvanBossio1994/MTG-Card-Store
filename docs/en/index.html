TCG Store Helper · Español

Your card store, organized in your own Google Sheet

A simple desktop app for small stores and collectors of Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards: stock, prices, cash register and reservations, with no databases or complicated systems. Your data stays in your Sheet.

Up-to-date stock

Search for a card, add or remove copies, and it is recorded in your Sheet.

Prices with your rule

Reference prices from public price lists, with your exchange rate, markup and rounding.

Clear cash register

Every sale and every cancellation is recorded, with daily and monthly totals.

Reservations and orders

Hold cards for a customer. If they are not picked up in time, they go back to stock automatically.

How it works

The app runs on your own computer. You sign in with your Google account and the app creates an inventory spreadsheet in your Google Drive. It only asks to see your email address and to access the files it creates itself, never the rest of your Drive.

Plans
