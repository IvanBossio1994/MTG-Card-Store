Your everyday store work, in one place

  • Up-to-date stock

    Search for a card, add or remove copies, and it is recorded in your Sheet.

  • Prices with your rule

    Reference prices from public price lists, with your exchange rate, markup and rounding.

  • Clear cash register

    Every sale and every cancellation is recorded, with daily and monthly totals.

  • Reservations and orders

    Hold cards for a customer. If they are not picked up in time, they go back to stock automatically.

How it works

  1. Download and open the app

    It runs on your own computer and opens in your browser. Nothing to install.

  2. Sign in with Google

    The app creates an inventory spreadsheet in your Google Drive. It only sees your email address and the files it creates itself, never the rest of your Drive.

  3. Add cards and sell

    Sync when you open it, search for cards, use + and −, and check the register at the end of the day.
